philosophy.md

$ cat philosophy.md

  • Empower, don't replace. AI should make engineers more capable, not less necessary. The goal is to take the toil off people's plates so they can spend more time on the problems that need judgment, creativity and care.
  • Engineers stay in the driver's seat. AI suggests, people decide and own the result.
  • Be transparent about it. Talk openly about what works, what doesn't and where the risks are, including security and quality. When things go wrong, give people the chance to course correct.
  • Lead with empathy and trust. I trust people to do great work, and I know everyone has something going on in their lives. Technology should make room for the human side of work, not squeeze it out.
  • Embrace failure. Make it safe to experiment and to get it wrong, share what we learn, and help each other level up.
